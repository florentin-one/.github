# Florentin One

What is Florentin One?

Florentin One is my unified approach to integrating everything I work on - strategy consulting through WeMake, large language models, and the development of the Model Context Protocol (MCP) - into a single framework for building and researching human-focused systems.

The central question driving this work:

> *how do we create AI that enhances human capabilities instead of replacing them? Automation substitutes human effort; augmentation amplifies it. My focus is on the latter, ensuring accessibility for everyone, not just well-funded organizations.*

## What I'm building toward

Systems that reduce cognitive load rather than redistributing it. Most tools add another surface to manage; I'm interested in the ones that recede into the background and hand people back their attention. That means starting from the real problem - cognitive overload - not from a solution looking for a use case.

Everything here is built in coherence with the EU AI Act and grounded in WeMake's [ethics guidelines](https://wemake.cx/legal/ethics/): human and environment before profit, equal opportunity, transparency, and responsibility that shows up operationally - in code, contracts, and support - not just on paper.

## Who I build for

Organizations in the DACH region - across regulated domains such as health, finance, industry, the public sector, and education - that hold a clear standard for quality, security, and ethics, and that are ready to work with AI operationally, not just talk about it. The common thread is measurable cognitive overload on knowledge workers and a willingness to treat augmentation as the standard, not the exception.

### Key Principles That Define My Work

- **Problem First:** Start with tangible issues, like cognitive overload or user-centric value, rather than jumping to solutions.
- **Augmentation over Automation:** Technology is a multiplier for human potential, not a substitute for it.
- **Systems over Tools:** Tools alone don't solve problems; rethinking the system does.
- **Open Process:** Encourage transparency, open participation, and collaborative feedback.
- **Outcome over Output:** Focus on meaningful impact rather than deliverables.
- **Responsibility is Operational:** Accountability must be visible in how things are built, supported, and maintained.

## What shapes the work

This work is rooted in systems thinking and complexity, antifragility in organizations, ethics in technology, and the intersection of cognitive science with human-computer interaction. It draws from open-source principles like governance and contribution culture, as well as security and cloud innovations such as Zero Trust and resilience. Research from Microsoft, McKinsey, and UC Irvine on cognitive load and productivity also informs my approach.
