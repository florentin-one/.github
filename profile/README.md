# Florentin One

**What is Florentin One?**

Florentin One is how I, [heyFlorentin](https://github.com/heyFlorentin), combine everything I work with — strategy consulting through [WeMake](https://wemake.cx), large language models, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) development — into a single place to build and research human-centered systems.

The question I keep returning to: how do we build AI that amplifies people instead of replacing them? Automation substitutes human effort. Augmentation multiplies human capacity. I build for the second one, and I try to make it available to everyone, not just organizations that can afford it.

## What I'm building toward

Systems that reduce cognitive load rather than redistributing it. Most tools add another surface to manage; I'm interested in the ones that recede into the background and hand people back their attention. That means starting from the real problem — cognitive overload — not from a solution looking for a use case.

Everything here is built in coherence with the **EU AI Act** and grounded in WeMake's [ethics guidelines](https://wemake.cx/legal/ethics/): human and environment before profit, equal opportunity, transparency, and responsibility that shows up operationally — in code, contracts, and support — not just on paper.

## Principles

- **Problem first** — Start with the real problem (cognitive overload), not with solutions.
- **Augmentation over automation** — Technology should amplify people, not replace them.
- **Systems over tools** — More tools are rarely the answer.
- **Open process** — Public, traceable, contributions welcome.
- **Outcome over output** — Impact beats delivery.
- **Responsibility is operational** — Visible in code, contracts, and support.

## What shapes the work

Systems thinking and complexity, antifragile organizations, ethics in technology, cognitive science and human-computer interaction. Practices from open-source communities (governance, reviews, contribution culture), security and cloud pioneers (Zero Trust, resilience), and teams that treat documentation like a product. Research on cognitive load and productivity from Microsoft, McKinsey, and UC Irvine.

I work in the open: RFCs, design docs, postmortems, and decision journals — because public process is both a quality filter and a way to teach.

The future of work isn't more tools. It's better systems. That's what I'm working on.
